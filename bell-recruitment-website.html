About

Meet Julie Bell — Founder & CEO

One of Northern Ireland's most experienced FMCG recruitment professionals, with over 35 years of combined industry and recruitment experience.

Bell Recruitment was founded in 1999 with a single ambition: to become Northern Ireland's leading FMCG recruitment specialist by combining deep industry knowledge with a genuinely personal approach.

Before launching the business, Julie spent a decade working inside the FMCG industry for brands including Pepsi, 7UP, Ballygowan Water, and Budweiser — spanning promotional activity, in-store merchandising, and national sales.

That hands-on experience means we understand not just what a great candidate looks like on paper, but how they perform on a route, in a depot, and in front of a buyer.
